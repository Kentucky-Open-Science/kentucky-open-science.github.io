Help wanted: the accessibility bounty board

This site is being brought up to WCAG 2.1 AA, the standard adopted by the ADA Title II web rule, one small task at a time. Each task has automated acceptance tests; a volunteer claims it, fixes it (with or without a coding agent), and a maintainer reviews the change. Merged fixes earn points on the leaderboard.

{{board.ready}} tasks are open to claim, {{board.leased}} are in progress, and {{board.accepted}} have been completed. See the bounty board.
